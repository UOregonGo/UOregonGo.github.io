UO-CS Graduate Research Open House

The UO-CS Graduate Research Open House will be held on May 21, 2026 in Deschutes Hall, the home of Department of Computer Science in the University of Oregon campus. It will provide a unique opportunity for graduate students to showcase their research and connect with faculty, staff, and peers. The event will center around a graduate student poster competition, accompanied by a guest speaker talk, PhD graduates panel, an award session, and more.

Attentance

All university faculty, staff, and students are welcome to attend the event. No registration required. All event will be held in Deschutes Hall on May 21, 2026 from 12 pm - 5 pm.

Call for Poster

Graduate students who are interested in participating in the poster competition are required to submit a poster. All submitted posters will have the opportuntiy of display in the Deschutes Hall and published on the event website to help increase the visibility of students' research. This will be a great opportunity for students to showcase their research and get feedback from faculty and peers. As part of the poster competition, the poster review committee will judge the posters and award prizes to the top 7 posters. The winners will be announced at the end of the event.

For more information for poster preparation and submission instructions, please refer to Call for Posters.

Best Poster Awards

Cash prizes will be awarded to up to 7 posters, with the following distribution. Note that all posters must meet minimum review standard to quanlify for awards. For details please refer to Poster Judge Rubrics.

  • 1st place award: $200 (1 poster)
  • 2nd place award: $100 (2 posters)
  • 3rd place award: $50 (4 posters)
